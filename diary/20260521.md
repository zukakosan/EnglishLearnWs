## 📝 文法・自然さフィードバック

### スペル
- **dairy → diary**: dairy は「乳製品」🐄。"Like I wrote in my **diary**, ..."
- **posion → position**（2か所）: "milk the **position**" / "in this **position**"
- **postion → position**: "**position**s up for grabs"
- **wethere → whether**
- **incresed → increased**
- **addtion → addition**
- **workthrough → walkthrough**: 一語で walkthrough。

### 文法・自然さ

| 原文 | 修正案 | 解説 |
|---|---|---|
| how I am wishy washy | how wishy-washy I am | 感嘆系の `how + 形容詞 + S + V` 語順。realize/notice と相性◎。 |
| I crave the room for improvement | I crave **room** for improvement | room（余地）は不可算なので無冠詞。`the` を付けると特定の部屋になってしまう。 |
| put myself in more dynamic standpoint | put myself in **a** more dynamic **position/environment** | 可算名詞には `a` 必須。`standpoint`（観点・立場）よりも `position` / `environment` の方が文脈に合う。 |
| has been the really comfort zone | has been **a real** comfort zone | `the really` は不自然。`a real + 名詞` で「まさに〜だ」を表す。 |
| Losing it means to lose my home. | Losing it **means losing** my home. | mean + 動名詞 で「〜することを意味する」。mean + to do は「〜するつもり」になる。 |
| even if I apply the postions up for grabs | even if I apply **for** the positions up for grabs | apply は自動詞、目的語の前に `for` が必要。 |
| I can't tell wethere I actually can obtain | I can't tell whether I **can actually** get | 助動詞 + 副詞の語順（can actually）。`obtain` は硬いので会話文脈なら `get/land` が自然。 |
| My mind is set in stone. | My mind is **made up**, / It's **set in stone**. | `set in stone` は通常「決定事項・ルール」が主語（"The plan is set in stone"）。「決心」なら `My mind is made up.` |
| as long as it's internal transfer | as long as it's **an** internal transfer | 可算名詞に `a/an`。 |
| Moving out of this company is the other way around. | **It's** the other way around **if I move out of this company**. | the other way around は主語節を受けるより、独立した文として置く方が自然。 |
| getting myself use to the English environment | getting myself **used** to the English environment | `be/get used to` は過去分詞 `used`。 |
| though clients are all the Japanese | though **the** clients are all Japanese | `the Japanese` だと「日本人全体」を指すので不自然。形容詞の Japanese で十分。 |

## 🌟 良かった点
- 学習中の難しめイディオム（`get back into the swing of things` / `don't hold your breath` / `lay the groundwork` / `the other way around`）を**自然な流れの中で**使えていて、丸暗記ではなく文脈理解で出せている証拠。
- 葛藤 → 結論への論理展開（"Not applying is the biggest risk for me." → "My mind is set in stone." → "The earlier..., the better."）が英語的に力強い。
- "Here is the thing:" の導入は会話的でとても自然 👍

## 🎯 次回のチャレンジ
- **work one's way up**（Active `-`、叩き上げで出世する）: internal transfer の話と相性抜群。
  - 例: `If I take this internal transfer, I can work my way up faster than starting over somewhere else.`
- **in the works**（Active `-`、進行中）: 進行中の応募・計画について。
  - 例: `A career switch is in the works — I'm just waiting for the right opening.`
