I went on a trip to my hometown to attend my gramma's 50th day party after her death. This is not for the first time in ages because we visted here last month for her funeral. However, every single time when I come here, it brings back memories. This time, it reminds me of time especially with gramma.

For lunch, we had Sawayaka's hamburg. There's nothing like that. It's very throwback to my childhood. Those were the days because I had no responsibility to survive. I spent time just playing with my friends. Recently I've realized how great my father is. He raised me greatly and dedicated his time with me. I respect him. He treated us to Sawayaka.

In my parents' house, I can be in my element. The thing really special this time is that I brought my dog here. As I expected, she had a mind of her own. She sometimes got out of hand, so one of us had to keep an eye on her. It's our responsibility because we dragged her along here.

We dropped by grand parents' house mother-side to introduce my partner. I was able to make them happier by my dog.

Anyway, I was able to put my feet up for real. We sticked together and enjoyed meals. Cards on table, I had a blast in spite of her death. My time with my family is close to my heart. My partner and my dog made my day. It's a big day.

## 📝 文法・自然さフィードバック

1. **"gramma's 50th day party after her death"** → "grandma's 50th-day memorial service"（法要は memorial service。party は祝い事に使う語）
2. **"This is not for the first time in ages"** → "This wasn't the first time in ages — we visited last month for her funeral."（時制を過去に + 構文を簡潔に）
3. **"we visted"** → "we **visited**"（タイポ）
4. **"every single time when I come here"** → "every single time I come here"（every time の後に when は不要）
5. **"it reminds me of time especially with gramma"** → "it especially reminds me of times with Grandma"（副詞の位置 + times 複数 + 固有名詞大文字）
6. **"There's nothing like that"** → "There's nothing like **it**"（直前の話題を受けるときは it が自然）
7. **"It's very throwback to my childhood"** → "It's **a real** throwback to my childhood"（throwback は名詞。a real / such a で修飾）
8. **"I had no responsibility to survive"** → "I had zero responsibilities back then"（to survive との組み合わせが不自然）
9. **"He raised me greatly"** → "He **did a great job** raising me"（raise を greatly で修飾するのは不自然）
10. **"dedicated his time with me"** → "dedicated his time **to** me"（dedicate A **to** B が正しい前置詞）
11. **"I can be in my element"** → "I **feel** in my element" / "I'm in my element"（can be はやや硬い）
12. **"grand parents' house mother-side"** → "my **grandparents'** house on my **mother's side**"（grandparents は一語 + on my mother's side が定型）
13. **"I was able to make them happier by my dog"** → "My dog made them really happy"（by → with、または主語を犬に）
14. **"We sticked together"** → "We **stuck** together"（stick - stuck - stuck の不規則変化）
15. **"Cards on table"** → "Cards on **the** table"（定冠詞 the が必要）
16. **"in spite of her death"** → "despite the sad occasion"（her death は直接的すぎる。柔らかく）
17. **"It's a big day"** → "It **was** a big day"（過去の出来事なので過去形に統一）
