Yesterday, I had a good time with members in the same department. In the morning, I had a meeting with one of newcomers. He had much more experiences than me, suprising me. There would be an event in the afternoon, so my manger bump up our 1:1 meeting to the earlier slot in the morning. I supposed that he would mention the review of the report on my achievement, but that didn't happen. I told him that I wouldn't participate in the event upcoming, so I might have threw him off. The report and event aside, we didn't have topics to discuss, since I have got a good thing going. I wish I could blow him away by my achievements. I feel cold feet for craving sales while supporting clients, but this might cause a hairy situation. Even it's a lost cause, my company forces my hands to creating milestones.

Aside from a few meetings in the morning, I wasn't in charge of attending meetings, so I have my head in the clouds. Then I put my mind to job searching. However, it's a bit difficult to find a position going hand in hand. I wanna avoid the situation where I take a beating. I'm more than happy to change my position. At the same time, I'm more than to end up losing my job. I had a blast in the afternoon by spacing out.

## レビュー 📖

### 1. 内容へのコメント 💬

1on1 のスケジュール繰り上げ、業績レビューが出てこなかったのは肩透かしでしたね。「The report and event aside, we didn't have topics to discuss, since I have got a good thing going」というのは、ある意味すごく良い状態だと思います！ 困りごとがないからこそ話すことがない、というのはマネージャーから見ても「順調」のサイン。とはいえ、craving sales と supporting clients の板挟みで cold feet になっている葛藤は伝わってきました。"forcing your hand" な会社の方針はモヤモヤしますよね。 🥲  
午後の job searching → spacing out → "had a blast"、振り幅すごい(笑) でも頭を切り替える時間って大事です。

### 4. 文法・自然さフィードバック 📝

- **時制・スペル**
  - 原文: "my manger bump up our 1:1 meeting" → 修正: "my **manager bumped up** our 1:1 meeting"
  - 原文: "I might have threw him off" → 修正: "I might have **thrown** him off"
  - 原文: "suprising me" → 修正: "**surprising** me"

- **不可算名詞**
  - 原文: "He had much more experiences than me" → 修正: "He had much more **experience** than me"（experience は通常不可算）

- **冠詞**
  - 原文: "one of newcomers" → 修正: "one of **the** newcomers"

- **接続詞・前置詞**
  - 原文: "Even it's a lost cause" → 修正: "**Even if** it's a lost cause"
  - 原文: "blow him away by my achievements" → 修正: "blow him away **with** my achievements"
  - 原文: "The report and event aside" → 修正: "**Aside from** the report and the event"（"X aside" も使えるが文頭ではやや硬い）

- **慣用句のコロケーション**
  - 原文: "I feel cold feet for craving sales" → 修正: "I **have/get cold feet about** going for sales"（feel ではなく have/get）
  - 原文: "my company forces my hands to creating milestones" → 修正: "my company **forces my hand** to **create** milestones"（hand は単数、to + 動詞原形）

- **語法**
  - 原文: "a position going hand in hand" → 修正: "a position **that fits my career goals**" / "a position **aligned with what I want to do**"
  - 原文: "I'm more than to end up losing my job" → 修正: "**The last thing I want is to** end up losing my job" / "**I really don't want to** end up losing my job"
  - 原文: "There would be an event in the afternoon" → 修正: "There **was going to be** an event in the afternoon"（過去視点なので would be でもOKだが was going to be がより自然）
  - 原文: "I wasn't in charge of attending meetings" → 修正: "I **didn't have many meetings to attend**"（in charge of は「責任を負う」なので参加には不自然）

### 5. 良かった点 🌟

- **`have one's head in the clouds` のリベンジ成功！** 前回 5/13 では "my heads" と複数にしてしまっていたのが、今回はしっかり単数で書けています 👏
- **`put one's mind to sth` の自然な流れ**: 「ぼーっとしていた → 仕事探しに本気で取り組んだ」という対比が綺麗で、フレーズの使い時として完璧
- **`hairy` の初挑戦**: 未評価フレーズを文章にねじ込めたのは大きな進歩
- **`have got a good thing going` を文脈にハメられた**: 「報告内容が特になかった理由」として論理的に繋がっている

### 6. 次回のチャレンジ 🎯

- **`go hand in hand` の正しい使い方**: 2つの物事を主語にする
  - 例: "Working from home and **getting cabin fever go hand in hand** for me."
  - 例: "Salary growth and responsibility usually **go hand in hand**."

- **`have/get cold feet` (慣用句、master未登録だが頻出)**: feel ではなく have/get で
  - 例: "I'm **getting cold feet** about applying for the sales position."

- **`forces sb's hand` の正しい構文**: 単数 hand + to + 動詞原形
  - 例: "The new deadline **forced my hand** to skip the testing phase."
