I got nervous today since I had to facilitate the workshop on Value Stream Mapping. I crave the attention from people, but at the same time, I'm shy person deep inside. Addtion to that, I easily got sweat in fron of audience, making me laughing stock. Moreover, when I'm in the situation, I'm likely to have my heads in the clouds. Maybe, my brain tries to take my mind off the severe situation. I hope I could fly under the rader, but a manager designated me as a facilitator. There was no way avoiding such a task.

As a matter of fact, I led a team composed of relatively familiar members, plus one newcomer. That's why I could keep our discussion casual, comfortable, relatable. The task to present our result to the audience came out of the woodwork, but the newcomer made the most of his experience and explained our result really well. By facilitating this discussion, I was able to get a chance to turn over a new leaf. I set the bar lower. To put it mildly, it was a great opportunity to learn lessons and get closer to each other.

Anyway, when I saw the newcomer's presentation, I did a double-take and admired.

### 4. 文法・自然さフィードバック 📝

- **原文**: I crave the attention from people, but at the same time, I'm shy person deep inside.  
  **修正案**: I crave attention from people, but at the same time, *I'm a shy person* deep inside.  
  → "the attention" の "the" は不要（抽象名詞として無冠詞）。"shy person" には **a** が必要。

- **原文**: Addtion to that, I easily got sweat in fron of audience, making me laughing stock.  
  **修正案**: *In addition* to that, I easily *break into a sweat in front of an audience,* making me *a* laughing stock.  
  → typo: "Addtion" → "In addition", "fron" → "front"。"get sweat" は不自然 → "break into a sweat" / "start sweating"。"audience" にも冠詞 an が必要。

- **原文**: Moreover, when I'm in the situation, I'm likely to have my heads in the clouds.  
  **修正案**: Moreover, when I'm in *that* situation, I'm likely to have *my head* in the clouds.  
  → "the situation" は具体的な状況を指すなら "that situation" が自然。"heads" は単数 "head" に。

- **原文**: Maybe, my brain tries to take my mind off the severe situation.  
  **修正案**: Maybe my brain *is trying* to take my mind off the *stressful* situation.  
  → "severe" は病気・痛みなどに使う形容詞。プレッシャーのある状況には "stressful" / "intense" が自然。

- **原文**: I hope I could fly under the rader, but a manager designated me as a facilitator.  
  **修正案**: I *was hoping to* fly under the *radar*, but *my* manager designated me as *the* facilitator.  
  → typo: "rader" → "radar"。過去の願望なので "was hoping to"。"the facilitator"（その役割の単独担当者）が自然。

- **原文**: There was no way avoiding such a task.  
  **修正案**: There was no way *of* avoiding such a task. / There was no avoiding it.  
  → "no way" の後は "of + 動名詞" か "to + 動詞原形"。

- **原文**: I led a team composed of relatively familiar members, plus one newcomer.  
  → ほぼOK。よりカジュアルにするなら "I was leading a team of people I knew fairly well, plus one newcomer."

- **原文**: That's why I could keep our discussion casual, comfortable, relatable.  
  **修正案**: That's why I *was able to* keep our discussion casual, comfortable, *and engaging.*  
  → "relatable"（共感できる）は人や経験に使うことが多い。議論の質を表すなら "engaging"（魅力的な）が自然。リスト末尾には "and" を入れる。

- **原文**: The task to present our result to the audience came out of the woodwork...  
  **修正案**: The task *of presenting* our *results* to the audience *came out of nowhere*...  
  → "task to present" より "task of presenting"。"result" は複数形 "results" が自然。come out of the woodwork は人主語向き。

- **原文**: By facilitating this discussion, I was able to get a chance to turn over a new leaf.  
  **修正案**: By facilitating this discussion, I *got* a chance to *step out of my comfort zone.*  
  → "was able to get" は冗長。turn over a new leaf は悪い習慣を改める意なので文脈にミスマッチ。

- **原文**: I set the bar lower.  
  → OK。"I lowered the bar for myself" でもより明確。

- **原文**: To put it mildly, it was a great opportunity to learn lessons and get closer to each other.  
  **修正案**: To put it mildly, it was a great opportunity to *learn a lot and bond with the team.*  
  → "learn lessons" は「教訓を得る」で重い。"learn a lot" / "pick up new things" が軽快。"get closer to each other" は OK だが、ビジネス文脈なら "bond" がスマート。

- **原文**: Anyway, when I saw the newcomer's presentation, I did a double-take and admired.  
  **修正案**: Anyway, when I saw the newcomer's presentation, I did a double-take *and was genuinely impressed.*  
  → "admire" は他動詞なので目的語が必要。自動詞的に終わらせるなら "was impressed" や "was blown away" を使う。
