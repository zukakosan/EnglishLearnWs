### 4. 文法・自然さフィードバック 📝

- **原文**: My work as an enterprise architect is stagnated.  
  **修正案**: My work as an enterprise architect *has stagnated.* / *is stagnating.*  
  → stagnate は自動詞。be stagnated より能動形が自然。

- **原文**: To step it up, I need more opportunity to learn lessons.  
  **修正案**: To step it up, I need more *opportunities to learn new things.* / *chances to learn and grow.*  
  → opportunity は可算で複数 opportunities が自然。"learn lessons" は「教訓を得る」で、文脈の「成長したい」とは少しずれる。

- **原文**: If I stay at this position for a while, I must get a stable and relatively calm life.  
  **修正案**: If I stay in this position for a while, *I'll have* a stable and relatively calm life.  
  → 「at」より「in this position」が自然。"must get" は義務感が出てしまうので "will have / can have" が文意に合う。

- **原文**: But I have to see just day in, day out.  
  **修正案**: But it would just be the same routine, day in, day out.  
  → 「見る」だと意味が浮く。「同じ日常の繰り返し」を明示。

- **原文**: I can take my mind off by just browsing job opportunities out there.  
  **修正案**: I can take my mind off *it* by just browsing job opportunities out there.  
  → off の後に「気を紛らわす対象」を入れる。

- **原文**: To be something else, I shoud move faster.  
  **修正案**: To stand out / To become exceptional, I *should* move faster.  
  → typo (shoud → should)。something else は感嘆用法に限定。

- **原文**: Is this a situation where I stand the test of time?  
  **修正案**: Is this a moment where I'm being put to the test? / Will I stand the test of time *if I stay here?*  
  → 主語が「人」だと違和感。「キャリアが時の試練に耐えるか」なら主語を career にすると自然。

- **原文**: Not to cry over spilt milk, decision should be made as early as possible.  
  **修正案**: Rather than crying over spilt milk, *the decision* should be made as early as possible. / There's no point crying over spilt milk—I should decide as early as possible.  
  → 倒置気味の "Not to..." より分かりやすい接続に。冠詞 the を補う。

- **原文**: Considering such kind of matters, I was having my head in the cloud.  
  **修正案**: Thinking about all this, my head was *in the clouds.*  
  → "such kind of matters" は不自然。"all this / these things" でOK。

### 6. 次回のチャレンジ 🎯

要注意フレーズから2つ：

- **bump sth up** (d4 #108): 「予定や数値を前倒し／引き上げる」。  
  例: *"Can you bump up our meeting to 2 pm? I have a conflict later."*  
  → 日記なら「上司に締切を前倒しされた」「給料が上がった」シーンで使える。

- **come out of the woodwork** (d4 #381): 「突然湧いて出る」。**the** を忘れずに。  
  例: *"Once I announced my job change, recruiters started coming out of the woodwork."*  
  → 転職を考えてるなら、次の日記でまさに使えそう！
