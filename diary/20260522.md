### 4. 文法・自然さフィードバック 📝

- "I had just been played recordings on some topics" → **"I just watched some recorded sessions on various topics"**（受動態が崩れている。録画を「再生される」ではなく「視聴する」） 
- "As long as I know" → **"As far as I know"**（イディオム取り違え。as long as は条件、as far as は範囲・程度）
- "infrasturcture" → **"infrastructure"**（スペル）
- "such an infrasturcture-side featuring event" → **"an event that focused on the infrastructure side"**（"featuring event" は不自然）
- "put old-fashion technologies" → **"put old-school technologies in the spotlight"** または **"showcase legacy technologies"**（"put" だけだと目的語が浮く）
- "through my lens" → 通じるが、より自然なのは **"from my perspective"** / **"the way I see it"**
- "That's just an imagining thing." → **"I'm just imagining things."**
- "put my mind on running" → **"put my mind to running"**
- "I end up running over 10 kilometers long" → **"I ended up running more than 10 kilometers."**（過去形 + long は不要）
- "run isolately" → **"run alone"** / **"run by myself"**（isolately は標準語ではない）
- "the difference in speed.I was able to..." → ピリオド後にスペース必須
- "the thing weighs on my mind is..." → **"the thing that weighs on my mind is..."**（関係代名詞 that が必要）
- "apply the SMC position" → **"apply for the SMC position"**（apply は通常 for を伴う）
- "the last room for thinking" → **"the last chance to think it through"** / **"my last window to think about it"**（"room" は物理的余地で、決断の時間には不自然）
