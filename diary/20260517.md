### 📝 文法・自然さフィードバック

1. **冠詞・前置詞の抜け**
   - 原文: "We talked our plan for today over yesterday night"
   - 修正案: "We talked **over** our plan for today **last night**"
   - → `talk sth over`（句動詞）。`yesterday night` は不自然 → `last night`

2. **語順・受動態**
   - 原文: "The plan supposed was to go on running..."
   - 修正案: "The plan **was supposed to be** to go for a run..."
   - → `be supposed to do` の語順。"go on running" は「走り続ける」の意になるので、朝活なら `go for a run` が自然

3. **miss out の前置詞**
   - 原文: "We missed out the chance"
   - 修正案: "We missed out **on** the chance" / または単に "We missed the chance"
   - → `miss out` は自動詞句。目的語を取るときは `on` が必須

4. **時制**
   - 原文: "We haven't visit there for a long time, but we were satisfied"
   - 修正案: "We **hadn't visited** there for a long time, but we were satisfied"
   - → 過去の出来事との対比なので過去完了。また visit は他動詞なので `there` ではなく単に "We hadn't visited it/the bakery" がより正確

5. **スペル & 冠詞**
   - 原文: "eldery people were carring Mikoshi"
   - 修正案: "**elderly** people were **carrying** **a** Mikoshi"
   - 原文: "an energetic mood in streets" → "...in **the** streets"

6. **#198 のより自然な言い回し**
   - 原文: "I can't put my finger on my motivation, but recently I feel I have no motivation for catching up."
   - 修正案: "I can't put my finger on **why I've been losing motivation lately**, but I feel like I have no energy to catch up on things."
   - → 節を取る形にすると一気に英語らしくなります

7. **副詞・スペル**
   - "learing English everyday" → "**learning** English **every day**"
   - → `everyday` は形容詞（日常の～）、`every day` は副詞（毎日）
