### 📝 文法・自然さフィードバック

1. **"today was a great weather"** → **"the weather was great today"**
   - weather は不可算名詞なので "a" は付けられない

2. **"It blowed me away"** → **"It blew me away"**
   - blow の過去形は blew（不規則動詞）

3. **"It costed more than 1500 yen"** → **"It cost more than 1,500 yen"**
   - cost の過去形は cost（不規則動詞、原形と同じ）

4. **"my father's collegue"** → **"my father's colleague"**
   - スペルミス（colleague）

5. **"hamburg restaurant"** → **"Hamburg steak restaurant"** or **"hambāgu restaurant"**
   - 英語で "hamburg" だけだとドイツの都市名。日本の「ハンバーグ」は "Hamburg steak" や "Salisbury steak" と表現する

6. **"I know the drill how to reserve our seat"** → **"I know the drill for getting a reservation"**
   - "know the drill" の後に "how to" は不自然（上記チェック参照）

7. **"This idea popped up in my partner's mind"** → **"This idea popped into my partner's mind"**
   - "pop up" = 突然現れる、"pop into one's mind" = 頭にふと浮かぶ。前置詞は **into**

8. **"It was really delicious business as usual"** → **"It was delicious as usual"**
   - "business as usual" は「通常運転・いつも通り」の意味だけど、"delicious business as usual" はつながりが不自然。シンプルに "as usual" でOK
