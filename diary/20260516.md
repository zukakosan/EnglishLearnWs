### 📝 文法・自然さフィードバック

- **"I had a free time"** → "I had **some** free time"（free time は不可算なので "a" は不要）
- **"I'm still a ramen person"** → OK だが、"Don't get me wrong, but" の "but" は不要 → `Don't get me wrong — I'm still a ramen person at heart.`
- **"I still have room for enjoyment ... by changing the menu to order"** → 不自然。→ `There's still room to enjoy a familiar restaurant by trying something new on the menu.`
- **"time flied"** → **time flew**
- **"to see festival in our residential area, which is really big in all over Japan"** → 主語欠落・冠詞・前置詞要修正。→ `In the evening, I went to see a festival in my neighborhood that's famous all over Japan.`（"big in all over Japan" は不自然、"famous throughout Japan" や "well-known across Japan" が自然）
- **"I downplayed this festival for 3 years since I moved in to this area"** → "moved **into** this area" / より自然には "since I moved here"。→ `I'd been downplaying this festival for the three years since I moved here.`（過去完了で「今まで軽視してきた」のニュアンス）
- **"got my foot in the door to realizing local cultures"** → `took my first step toward appreciating local culture`（culture は通常単数で「文化全般」）

### 🌟 良かった点

- **`downplay sth` と `run sth by sb` を日記の中で自然に消化**できているのが素晴らしい。今日テストで×だったフレーズを即日アウトプットで挽回しているのは理想的な学習ループ！
- **`have a blast` を1日の冒頭と中盤で2回使う**構成、ストーリーテリングとして上手い。
- **比喩的な `can't afford to`**（高音を歌う余裕がない）の使い方が単なる「お金がない」を超えてネイティブっぽい。

### 🎯 次回のチャレンジ

- **`live for sth`**: 「〜が生きがい」。名詞を直接続けるのがコツ。例: `Back in college, I lived for karaoke nights with my friends.`
- **`get one's foot in the door`**: キャリア/組織系の「足がかり」で使うと◎。例: `Joining that volunteer team was how I got my foot in the door of the local community.`
