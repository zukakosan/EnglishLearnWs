
I watched a reveiw video about a book, which is "DIE WITH ZERO". That video gave me an eye-opening moment. Actually, I read the same book back in 2022, I guess. However, I've forgotten the attitude to my life learned from that book. Recently, I'm looking for the next job opportunity. I feel like this is because I feel a stagnation and being in autopilot. I should be in charge of my life more. 

If you think about it, I started running routine. This is the unintentional result of avoiding the autopilot. I don't intend to do a lost cause but I should stimulate my life more strongly. Learning English is the same thing. I know the power of English to expand my life experiences. I need to feel out what I live for. I should run this topic by my partner to plan our life in near future. I can't put my finger on my life, but I've realized that the more I experience, the happier I will become. 

Sometimes I might have swept my dream under the carpet, and rein what I wanted to do, hesitated to weigh in...These are the worst attetude to my life. My life and work are the different kettle of fish, but related to each other heavily. Do you think I can get my foot in the door? I promise I'll stop downplaying the future plan of my life.

## レビュー（2026-05-18）

### 1. 日記の内容へのコメント 💬

『DIE WITH ZERO』を再読じゃなくて「レビュー動画」で思い出すって、人生の節目では本当によくありますよね。2022年に読んだ本が今のあなたに別の意味で響いたという気づき、すごく良いタイミングだと思います。"stagnation" と "autopilot" という言葉に、悶々と何かを感じている今のリアルがにじんでます。

ランニングを始めたのが「無意識のオートパイロット回避だった」という分析、面白いです！ 体が先に動いていたんですね。転職や人生設計についてパートナーと話すというのも、まさに「自分の人生を握る」第一歩。次回はぜひ「どんな経験を増やしたいか」具体例を1つ書いてみてください 🙂

### 4. 文法・自然さフィードバック 📝

- **冠詞・複数形**
  - 原文: "I started running routine."
  - 修正: "I started **a** running routine."
- **時制の整合**
  - 原文: "Recently, I'm looking for the next job opportunity."
  - 修正: "Recently, I've **been** looking for **my** next job opportunity."（`recently` は現在完了進行形が自然）
- **前置詞**
  - 原文: "the attitude to my life"
  - 修正: "the attitude **toward** my life"（または `the mindset about life`）
- **冠詞・所有格**
  - 原文: "I feel like this is because I feel a stagnation and being in autopilot."
  - 修正: "I feel like this is because I sense **stagnation** and **that I'm on autopilot**."（`in autopilot` ではなく **`on autopilot`** が定型）
- **語順・自然さ**
  - 原文: "I don't intend to do a lost cause but I should stimulate my life more strongly."
  - 修正: "I don't intend to chase a lost cause, but I **want to bring more excitement into** my life."（`stimulate my life strongly` は不自然）
- **スペル**
  - "reveiw" → **review**
  - "attetude" → **attitude**
- **文の自然化**
  - 原文: "the power of English to expand my life experiences"
  - 修正: "**how English can broaden my life experiences**"（より口語的）
- **時制と語順**
  - 原文: "Sometimes I might have swept my dream under the carpet, and rein what I wanted to do, hesitated to weigh in..."
  - 修正: "Sometimes I might have swept my dreams under the carpet, **reined in** what I wanted to do, **and** hesitated to weigh in..."（動詞の時制を揃え、`and` で列挙）

### 5. 良かった点 🌟

- 一つのテーマ（人生のオートパイロット脱却）を軸に、要注意フレーズを **10 個**も組み込めました。これは過去最高クラスの密度です！
- `run this topic by my partner` / `sweep my dream under the carpet` / `hesitated to weigh in` / `stop downplaying` は完璧な使い方。文脈にぴたっとはまっています。
- "the more I experience, the happier I will become" の比較級構文も自然で良かったです。

### 6. 次回のチャレンジ 🎯

今日 △ だったフレーズを **次の日記でリベンジ**してみましょう:

- **rein in**: 「衝動／支出／部下／自分」を抑制する文脈で使うと自然。
  - 例: *I'm trying to **rein in** my urge to switch jobs impulsively.*
- **a different kettle of fish**: AとBが「別問題」と対比する形が定型。
  - 例: *Reading about a new career is one thing, but actually making the move is **a different kettle of fish**.*
- **can't put my finger on**: 抽象的な対象ではなく「何が」を具体化して使う。
  - 例: *Something feels off about my routine, but I **can't put my finger on** what it is.*
